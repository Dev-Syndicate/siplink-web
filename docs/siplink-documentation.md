# 1. Company Positioning
SIPLINK Communications provides IP voice, VoIP, SIP Trunking, Hosted PBX, Business VoIP, Contact Center, Unified Communications, connectivity, network and infrastructure solutions for businesses. SIPLINK can design and deploy communications environments for small offices, multi-site organizations, remote teams, high-volume contact centers and service-provider-oriented requirements.
Our solutions bring together business telephony, intelligent call routing, IVR, contact-center capabilities, recording, analytics, mobile and browser communications, messaging, CRM integrations, security, redundancy and automation.
