## Shared art direction

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: SipLink website right-side hero. Scene: seamless white studio with subtle blush shadows. Style: premium realistic 3D render; white porcelain and translucent blush pink glass, soft diffused daylight, subtle glass refraction. Composition: square airy balanced sculpture, fully visible with generous margins. Constraints: no words, no letters, no logos, no watermark.
